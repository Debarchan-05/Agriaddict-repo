What is the validity of the Master Course?

The course remains valid up to the examination day.

What is the difference between Target and Foundation Batch?

Target Batch is valid until the current-year examination. Foundation Batch is valid until the next-year examination.

Is Mock Boost included in the Master Course?

Yes, Mock Boost is included in the Master Course.

How can I access the course after purchasing it?

Log in using the same email used for purchase, then open the course from your Library or Dashboard.

How can I clarify subject or material doubts?

How can I join the WhatsApp Master Group?

Enrolled students receive a Google Form through WhatsApp and receive group access after completing the process.

How can I report a mistake in a mock test?
